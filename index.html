Privacy Policy: Gladiator's Revenge

Effective date: 24 October 2026

Who we are

Gladiator's Revenge is made by Dash Games, a one-person studio run by Daniel Ashush. "We" in this policy means Dash Games.

Privacy contact: dashgamesindie@gmail.com

This policy covers the game Gladiator's Revenge on Google Play (Android) and on Steam (Windows), including the free Steam demo. Google, Valve, Discord and the social networks where we post have their own privacy policies.

In short

  • Your save and your settings stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number.
  • Play data goes to Unity Analytics only if you say yes to the "Share data?" question. You can change your answer in the settings at any time.
  • On Android, the free game shows an ad between chapters, through Unity's LevelPlay. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form first asks whether ads may use your data. Owners of the Champions Pack see no ads.
  • On Android, Google Play takes care of the Champions Pack payment. We never see your payment details.
  • On Steam, Valve takes care of achievements, and the game shows your Steam name on your gladiator. That name stays on your computer.

1. What stays on your device

The game keeps these on your phone or computer, and never sends them to us:

  • the save file of your current run;
  • your settings (language, volume, auto save, touch layout, key bindings, map hints, tutorial, easy mode);
  • the characters, challenge levels and achievements you have unlocked;
  • whether you own the Champions Pack (Android) and whether Google has confirmed the purchase;
  • your answers to the "Share data?" question and to Google's ads consent form;
  • how many runs you have lost (for an achievement, and on Android to offer the easy mode).

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your device's settings, deletes all of it.

On Android, if you have Android's backup switched on, Android may include the game's files in your Google account's backup. That backup belongs to your Google account; we cannot see it.

2. Play data (Unity Analytics): only with your consent

The first time you start the game it asks "Share data?". Only if you answer yes does the game start Unity Analytics, a service of Unity Technologies, and send it:

  • What happens in the game: a run starts (which gladiator, challenge level, whether you skipped the tutorial); a fight or arena ends (chapter, which fight, won or lost, your level, how long it took, lives left); a run ends (won or died, chapter, level); you pick or buy an item (talisman, skill, equipment or potion, its name, where it came from, its price in game gold); you open the Champions Pack window; an ad is shown (Android); you buy the Champions Pack (Android, sent by Unity's purchase system).
  • What Unity's SDK adds by itself: a random installation ID that the SDK creates on your device; the start and end of each play session and a signal every minute while you play; the game's version; your device's model, operating system, processor, graphics chip, memory, screen size and resolution, battery level, language and country setting. Unity also receives your IP address, which it uses to work out your country.

We use this to see which fights are too hard, which items players pick and where they stop, so we can balance and improve the game. We do not use it for advertising and do not try to find out who you are. The installation ID is not linked to your name, email, Google account or Steam account.

Unity processes this data on our behalf, as our service provider (a "processor" under EU law). Unity stores it on servers in Belgium and the Netherlands, with a copy in the United States, and keeps the raw event data for 13 months. Unity's policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Changing your mind: switch "Share data?" off in the game's settings. Nothing more is sent from then on. Because the game never knows who you are, we cannot pick out and delete the play data already sent; Unity deletes the raw events after 13 months.

3. Ads (Android only)

The free Android game shows a full-screen ad when you sail to the next chapter. When the ad closes you get a small in-game reward, whether you watched it to the end or not.

The ads come through Unity LevelPlay, Unity's ad mediation service, from the ad networks Unity Ads and ironSource Ads (both run by Unity Technologies). To show ads, count and measure them, limit how often you see the same one, and prevent fraud, these networks collect data from your device, such as your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, your IP address and approximate location (from the IP address), your device's type and settings, and how you interact with the ads. They use this data under their own privacy policies, as independent companies: Unity (Unity Ads, ironSource Ads, LevelPlay): https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: before the first ad, Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) asks whether the ad networks may use your data for personalised ads. Your answer is kept on your device and passed to the ad networks. If you say no, you see ads that are not personalised. You can change your answer at any time with the "Ad privacy" button in the game's settings. Google's policy for the form: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Your other choices:

  • Your device's ad settings: you can reset or delete your advertising ID and turn off personalised ads in Android's settings (for example Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the phone).
  • The Champions Pack removes all ads; the game then does not start the ads at all.

The Steam version has no ads.

4. The Champions Pack (Android only)

You buy the Champions Pack through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details under its own terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy, https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/); we never see them. Google tells the game that your Google account owns the pack, so it comes back after you reinstall.

The game talks to Google Play through Unity's purchase system (Unity IAP). Unity IAP sends Unity a report of each purchase step: the product, the order details Google Play returns, your device's model, operating system and language, and an installation ID. Unity uses it to run and report on the purchase service and keeps it for 90 days. This report is sent whether or not you agreed to share play data.

On Steam, the demo has no purchases; when the full game is sold on Steam, Valve handles that purchase.

5. Steam

The Steam version uses Valve's Steamworks:

  • Achievements: when you unlock one, the game tells Steam, and Valve stores it on your Steam account, where it can show on your Steam profile.
  • Your Steam name: the game reads your Steam display name from the Steam app on your computer and writes it on your gladiator's name tag and in the fight screen. We do not send it anywhere.
  • Wishlist button: opens the game's Steam store page.

As for every game on Steam, Valve gives us anonymous reports about sales and play, and the information the Steamworks API provides about players of our game (at least that you own it, and your achievements). Valve's privacy policy: https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/

6. Links to other sites

The game links to our Discord server, the game's Steam store page and this policy. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

7. If you email us

If you write to us, we get your email address and what you write. We use it only to answer you and to fix what you report, and delete it once the matter is closed.

8. Your rights and how to ask

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you can ask what data about you is held, have it corrected or deleted, object to its use, and withdraw your consent at any time. Email dashgamesindie@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request" and say which platform you play on.

  • Play data (Unity Analytics): switch "Share data?" off in the settings to stop it. We can ask Unity on your behalf, but because the game never knows who you are, we may not be able to find your data.
  • Ads data: change your answer with "Ad privacy" in the settings, reset your advertising ID in Android's settings, or write to Unity at DPO@unity3d.com.
  • Purchase data: Google (https://policies.google.com/privacy); the purchase reports of Unity IAP: unity-iap-contact@unity3d.com.
  • Steam data: Valve, through Steam Support (help.steampowered.com > My Account > Data Related to Your Steam Account).

We answer within one month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Legal bases (EU and UK)

  • Play data: your consent, which you can withdraw in the settings.
  • Ads: personalised ads only with your consent in Google's form; ads that are not personalised on the basis of our legitimate interest in funding the free game.
  • The Champions Pack: to give you what you bought (contract).
  • Emails: to answer you (legitimate interest).

10. International transfers

Unity Technologies, Google and Valve are based in the United States and process data in the US and other countries. They protect such transfers with the safeguards the law requires (for example standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework), as their own policies describe.

11. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete the game or its data.
  • Unity Analytics: raw events 13 months; summary figures until deleted.
  • Unity IAP purchase reports: 90 days.
  • Emails: until the matter is closed.
  • The ad networks, Google and Valve: as their own policies say.

12. Children

Gladiator's Revenge is made for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or under the age of digital consent where you live). If you are a parent and think your child has sent us data, email us and we will ask for it to be deleted.

13. Security

The services named above send data over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. We run no server and keep no database of players.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we update the date at the top, and for an important change we say so on the game's store pages.
